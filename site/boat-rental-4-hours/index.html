Why 4 Hours? The Perfect Duration

A 4-hour boat charter in Marbella is the most popular duration for a reason. It's long enough to explore the coastline, enjoy swimming and snorkeling, have lunch, and relax — but not so long that you're fatigued. Most groups agree: 4 hours is the sweet spot.

What Happens in Your 4 Hours?

  • 0:00 – 0:45 — Board at Puerto Banús, safety briefing, cruise to first destination
  • 0:45 – 2:15 — Swimming, snorkeling, or paddleboarding at scenic cove
  • 2:15 – 3:15 — Lunch, drinks, relax on deck
  • 3:15 – 4:00 — Cruise back to Puerto Banús

Timing is flexible — your skipper will customize based on your group's preferences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 4 hours long enough for a boat charter?

Yes! 4 hours is the sweet spot for most groups. You get quality time on the water, enjoy activities, and return before fatigue sets in. Most guests say 4 hours felt just right.

What if we want longer than 4 hours?

8-hour and overnight charters are available. 8-hour charters cost roughly 1.8–2x the 4-hour price. Overnight charters start at €1,500–€2,000/night depending on boat.

Can we customize the 4-hour itinerary?

Absolutely. Your skipper will tailor the route, stops, and activities to your group's preferences. Want more time snorkeling? Skip the lunch? Different cove? Just tell us during booking.

What happens if the weather is bad during our 4-hour charter?

Safety first. If conditions are unsafe, we'll offer a reschedule to a better day with full refund. Weather cancellations are 100% refundable.

Do kids enjoy 4-hour boat charters?

Yes! Kids typically love the swimming, snorkeling, and skipper attention. 4 hours is ideal for families with children — long enough to be fun, short enough to stay engaged.

What's the best time to book a 4-hour charter?

Morning charters (8am–12pm) or afternoon charters (2pm–6pm) are popular. Sunset charters (5pm–9pm) are premium. Book 3–7 days ahead for best availability.
