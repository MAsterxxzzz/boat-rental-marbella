Booking Process & Payment

No deposits required. Prices are confirmed once you book. Payment due upon arrival before departure.

Payment methods: Cash (EUR), Card (Visa/Mastercard), Bank transfer, Crypto (BTC/ETH/USDT)

Cancellation policy: Full refund up to 7 days before charter. 50% refund 3–6 days before. No refund within 48 hours. Weather cancellations are always 100% refundable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a deposit or upfront payment required?

No. Prices are quoted upfront with no deposit. You pay on the day of your charter, just before boarding.

Do prices include skipper and fuel?

Yes. Every charter includes a professional licensed skipper, fuel for the standard route, drinks, and insurance. It's all included in the price shown.

What happens if I want to stay longer than 8 hours?

Overnight and multi-day charters are available at daily rates starting around €1,500–€2,000/day depending on boat. Contact us on WhatsApp for overnight quotes.

Can you give me a discount for booking multiple days?

Yes. Multi-day bookings typically qualify for 10–15% discounts. Ask us directly on WhatsApp.

Are there extra charges for large groups?

No extra charges for groups up to the boat's capacity. All group sizes pay the same hourly rate (shared across the group).

Do you accept payment plans?

For bookings over €2,000, we can arrange 50/50 payment split: half at booking, half on charter day.
