Skipper FAQ

Do I need a sailing license?

No. With a skipper included, you don't need any boating license. The skipper is certified to operate the boat. You're free to just enjoy the day.

Can the skipper teach me to sail?

Yes. Many guests enjoy learning basics from our skippers. Let us know during booking if you want sailing instruction, and we'll match you with an experienced skipper.

Are skippers multilingual?

All skippers speak English. Many also speak German, Dutch, French, Spanish, or Italian. Request a specific language during booking.

What if I'm nervous about being on a boat?

Our skippers are experienced with nervous guests and anxious first-timers. They'll explain everything, go slow, and adjust the experience for your comfort. It's very safe.

Can the skipper recommend the best activities?

Absolutely. Skippers know the best anchoring spots for snorkeling, where to see dolphins, quietest beaches, and current weather/sea conditions. They'll customize your day.

Is the skipper tip included in the price?

No, tipping is not included but is appreciated (10% typical for excellent service). It's entirely optional.
