Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer overnight luxury yacht charters?

Yes! Overnight charters available on all luxury superyachts. Rates start at €2,500–€3,800/night including crew, cabins, and catering. Multi-day charters available with itinerary planning.

Can I host a wedding or proposal on a luxury yacht?

Absolutely! We've hosted dozens of special celebrations. We can coordinate catering, flowers, photography, music, and crew service for an unforgettable moment.

What's the cancellation policy for luxury charters?

Full refund up to 14 days before charter. 50% refund 7–13 days before. No refund within 7 days (unless weather-related). Premium charters may have different terms — discuss during booking.

Do you offer crew services separately?

Yes! If you prefer to charter our yacht with your own crew, we can arrange that. Pricing varies based on vessel and arrangement. Contact us for details.

Are multi-day Mediterranean charters available?

Yes! We arrange multi-day charters to nearby islands (Balearics, Moroccan coast). Crew, catering, and all logistics handled. Contact us for custom itineraries.
