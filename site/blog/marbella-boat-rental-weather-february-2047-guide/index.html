February in Marbella offers a surprising window for a comfortable winter boat rental. With average highs of 17°C, 6 hours of sunshine and calm Mediterranean seas, it's an ideal time to explore the Costa del Sol without the summer crowds. Whether you're planning a birthday celebration, a corporate outing, or simply a relaxing day on the water, this guide covers everything you need to know about February boat rental Marbella 2047 — from weather averages and sea conditions to the best boats and destinations.

February Weather in Marbella: What to Expect

February in Marbella is mild and generally pleasant. Average daytime highs reach 17°C, dropping to around 10°C in the evenings. You can expect about 6 hours of sunshine each day, with occasional rain (typically 5-7 rainy days in the month). The sea temperature hovers around 15°C, so swimming is possible but brisk. Winds average 10-15 knots, and wave heights are usually 0.5-1 metre — perfect for a stable and comfortable charter.

Sea Conditions for February Boating

The Mediterranean along Marbella's coast is generally calm in February. The prevailing winds are from the west or northwest, keeping the sea state moderate. Swell rarely exceeds 1 metre, making it suitable for all boat sizes in our fleet. For reference, the Boat Rental Marbella fleet includes everything from the 6.4m Mariah SX21 to the 29m Ferretti 94, all capable of handling typical February conditions. Always check the forecast on the day, but winter charters are regularly enjoyed without issue.

Where to Go: February Destinations from Puerto Banús

Winter cruising offers quieter anchorages and fewer boats. Popular February routes include:

  • Cabopino (30 min east) — a sandy beach with a protected cove, great for a lunch stop.
  • Cala del Faro — a sheltered rocky cove near Marbella, ideal for anchoring.
  • Estepona (45 min west) — a charming marina with excellent seafood restaurants.
  • Sotogrande (1 hour west) — luxury marina and golf resort, suitable for a full-day trip.

All departures are from Puerto Banús, the iconic marina on the Golden Mile. Your licensed skipper will tailor the route to the day's weather and your preferences.

Packing Tips for a February Boat Trip

Layers are your friend: a t-shirt, a fleece or jumper, and a windproof jacket. Non-slip shoes are recommended. Sunglasses and sunscreen are still necessary — the UV is strong even in winter. A hat and gloves can be useful for the breeze. The boat's cabin provides warmth, so you can easily step inside to warm up. Bring a camera for the stunning views of La Concha mountain and the coastline.

Frequently asked questions

What is the weather like for a boat rental in Marbella in February 2047?

February in Marbella averages highs of 17°C and lows of 10°C. Expect around 6 hours of sunshine daily, with occasional rain. The sea temperature is about 15°C, so bring a light jacket or jumper for the breeze. Winds are typically 10-15 knots, and seas are calm at 0.5-1m — perfect for a comfortable charter.

Can I swim during a February boat charter in Marbella?

Swimming is possible but the sea is cool at 15°C. Most guests prefer to enjoy the sun on deck or anchor in sheltered coves like Cala del Faro. If you do swim, a wetsuit is recommended. The calm conditions make it safe for a quick dip, but it's not the warmest time of year for extended swimming.

Which boats are best for a February rental in Marbella?

For a February charter, the Astondoa 40 or Azimut 39 (Tier A) are excellent value at €749/2h to €2,299/8h, with enclosed saloons for warmth. The larger Mangusta 80 (€4,719/4h, free jet ski) offers a flybridge with wind protection. All boats have heating and indoor seating areas.

What destinations are recommended for a February boat trip from Puerto Banús?

Popular winter routes include cruising past the Golden Mile to Cabopino (30 min), anchoring at Cala del Faro for lunch, or heading east towards Estepona (45 min). The coastline is quieter in February, so you can enjoy a more private experience. Sotogrande is also reachable for a longer day trip.

What should I wear for a boat rental in Marbella in February?

Layered clothing is key: a t-shirt, a fleece or jumper, and a windproof jacket. Non-slip shoes are recommended. Sunglasses and sunscreen are still useful as the sun is bright. Bring a hat and gloves if you're sensitive to the breeze — the boat's cabin provides warmth when needed.

How many hours should I book for a February charter in Marbella?

A half-day (4 hours) is popular for a relaxed cruise and lunch stop. Full-day (8 hours) allows you to explore further, like to Estepona or Sotogrande. For a sunset trip, 2-hour options are available. February sun sets around 18:45, so plan accordingly.

Is February a good time for dolphin watching in Marbella?

Yes, February is a good month for dolphin sightings along the Marbella coast. Common dolphins and bottlenose dolphins are often seen near Cabopino or off the coast of Estepona. The calmer seas make spotting easier. No guarantees, but many charters report sightings during winter trips.
