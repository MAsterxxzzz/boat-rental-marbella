When choosing between the Azimut 39 and the Fairline Targa 12m for a Marbella charter, fuel cost is a key factor. Both boats depart from Puerto Banús and offer similar lengths (12.5m vs 12m), but their fuel consumption differs noticeably. The Azimut 39 burns 80–100 litres per hour at cruise, while the Fairline Targa 12m uses 90–110 L/h. With diesel at €1.50–1.70/L in 2027, that gap adds up over a day on the water. This guide breaks down the real numbers so you can choose the most cost-effective option for your Marbella boat trip.

Azimut 39 vs Fairline Targa 12m: Key Specs

Both boats are popular choices for day charters in Marbella, but they have different strengths. The Azimut 39 is a sleek flybridge cruiser known for its fuel efficiency and spacious cockpit. The Fairline Targa 12m is a sporty open-cockpit model with a deeper V-hull for better rough-water handling. Here's how they compare:

  • Length: Azimut 39 (12.5m) vs Fairline Targa 12m (12m)
  • Passenger capacity: Azimut 39 (11 pax) vs Fairline Targa 12m (10 pax)
  • Engine: Both twin diesel inboards (typically Volvo Penta or similar)
  • Cruise speed: ~22–26 knots for both
  • Departure: Both from Puerto Banús, Marbella

Which Boat Saves You More on Fuel?

If fuel economy is your priority, the Azimut 39 is the clear winner. It burns 10–20% less fuel per hour, carries one more passenger, and has a lower base charter rate. That said, the Fairline Targa 12m offers a sportier ride and better rough-water capability, which may be worth the extra cost if you plan to go further offshore or in choppier conditions. For most Marbella day trips — hugging the coast from Puerto Banús to Estepona or Cala del Faro — the Azimut 39 is the more cost-effective choice.

Real-World Fuel Savings: A Day on the Water

Imagine a full-day charter from Puerto Banús to Cala Cortés and back, cruising at 24 knots. The Azimut 39 uses ~720 litres (€1,152), while the Fairline Targa 12m uses ~800 litres (€1,280). That €128 saving covers lunch at a beach club or a few extra drinks. If you're chartering for a group of 10, the per-person fuel cost drops to €115 for the Azimut vs €128 for the Fairline — a noticeable difference when budgeting.

Frequently asked questions

Which boat is more fuel-efficient, the Azimut 39 or the Fairline Targa 12m?

The Azimut 39 is slightly more fuel-efficient, burning 80–100 L/h at cruise vs the Fairline Targa 12m's 90–110 L/h. Over a 2-hour charter, you save roughly €30–34 on fuel with the Azimut.

What is the fuel cost for a half-day charter on the Azimut 39 in Marbella?

A half-day (4-hour) charter on the Azimut 39 burns about 320–400 litres of diesel. At €1.60/L average, fuel costs €512–640. This is included in the Tier A price of €1,229 (4-hour charter).

Does the Fairline Targa 12m have a higher fuel cost than the Azimut 39?

Yes. The Fairline Targa 12m uses 90–110 L/h vs the Azimut's 80–100 L/h. Over an 8-hour day, the Fairline burns 720–880 litres (€1,152–1,408) vs the Azimut's 640–800 litres (€1,024–1,280).

Are fuel costs included in the charter price for these boats?

Yes. For both the Azimut 39 (Tier A: €749/2h → €2,299/8h) and the Fairline Targa 12m (pricing on request), fuel, skipper, drinks, snacks, and VAT are all included. No hidden fuel surcharges.

What is the range of the Azimut 39 vs the Fairline Targa 12m on a full tank?

The Azimut 39 carries ~800L fuel, giving a range of ~180–200 NM at cruise. The Fairline Targa 12m holds ~900L, offering ~200–220 NM. Both easily cover Marbella to Estepona and back with reserve.

Which boat is better for a fuel-conscious charterer in Marbella?

The Azimut 39 is the better choice for fuel economy, saving €60–80 on an 8-hour day vs the Fairline Targa 12m. It also carries 11 passengers (vs 10) and costs less per hour in Tier A pricing.

How does sea state affect fuel consumption for these boats in Marbella?

In calm conditions (typical Marbella summer), both burn at the lower end of their range. In choppy seas (e.g., Poniente wind), consumption can rise 15–20%. The Fairline's deeper V-hull handles rougher seas with less speed loss.

Can I charter the Fairline Targa 12m for a full day and what is the fuel cost?

Yes. The Fairline Targa 12m is available for full-day charters from Puerto Banús. Fuel cost (included) for an 8-hour cruise at cruise speed is ~€1,152–1,408, based on 90–110 L/h and €1.60/L diesel.
