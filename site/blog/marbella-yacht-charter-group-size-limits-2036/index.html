Planning a boat trip in Marbella for 2036? The first thing to know is how many people you can bring. Every vessel in our Puerto Banús fleet has a strict maximum passenger count – including the skipper – and you cannot exceed it. Whether you're organising a birthday celebration, a corporate outing, or a family day on the water, understanding these group size limits for boat rental in Marbella 2036 will save you hassle and help you pick the right boat. Below we break down the capacities for each of our yachts, answer common questions, and give you the facts you need to book confidently.

Why group size limits exist

Group size limits aren't arbitrary – they're set by the boat's CE certification, insurance policy, and Spanish maritime regulations. Each vessel is designed and tested to carry a maximum number of people safely, considering stability, life raft capacity, and emergency evacuation. The skipper is legally responsible for enforcing this limit. Overloading a boat can result in fines, voided insurance, and – most importantly – risk to everyone on board. That's why we never bend the rules, even for small children.

How to choose the right boat for your group size

Start with your guest count. If you have 5 people or fewer, the Dubhe or Mariah SX21 are affordable options. For 6–8 guests, consider the Bandido or Red Tide. Groups of 9–10 fit well on the Fairline Targa, Pershing 46, or Lagoon 380. If you're 10–12, the Azimut 58 Flybridge or any of the 24m+ yachts are ideal. Remember: the skipper counts in the total, so if you want all 10 guests to be your friends, choose a boat rated for 11 or 12 pax.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum group size for a boat rental in Marbella in 2036?

The maximum is 12 passengers (including the skipper) on our largest yachts: Mangusta 80, Azimut 58 Flybridge, Canados 86, Ferretti 94, K80, and Maiora 26m. Smaller boats carry 5–11 guests. We cannot exceed these numbers – it's a safety and insurance requirement.

Can I split my group across two boats?

We recommend booking one larger yacht for 12 pax rather than splitting. If you have more than 12 guests, you can charter two boats from our fleet (e.g., two Azimut 58s). Contact us via WhatsApp for a custom quote – we'll coordinate departure times so you cruise together.

Do children count towards the group size limit?

Yes. Every person on board, regardless of age, counts towards the maximum passenger number. Infants, toddlers, and children are included. There are no exceptions for babies, even if they sit on a lap. This is a legal requirement under Spanish maritime regulations.

What happens if we show up with more people than allowed?

We cannot let you board if your group exceeds the boat's certified capacity. The skipper will refuse departure. To avoid disappointment, confirm your final guest count at least 48 hours before departure. If you need to add passengers, we may be able to upgrade you to a larger vessel (subject to availability).

Does the skipper count in the group size?

Yes. The maximum passenger number includes the skipper. For example, an Azimut 39 rated for 11 pax means 10 guests + 1 skipper. Our pricing includes the skipper, so you don't need to worry about an extra person – just remember that the total headcount on board cannot exceed the stated limit.

Which Marbella boat is best for a group of 10 adults?

For exactly 10 guests, we recommend the Fairline Targa 12m (10 pax including skipper – so 9 guests + skipper) or the Pershing 46 (10 pax total – 9 guests + skipper). If you want all 10 as guests, choose the Azimut 39 (11 pax total – 10 guests + skipper) or the Lagoon 380 (10 pax total – 9 guests + skipper).

Can I rent a boat for a party of 15 in Marbella?

Not on a single vessel – our largest yachts carry a maximum of 12 passengers including skipper. For 15 people, you would need two boats. We can arrange that: for example, a Mangusta 80 (12 pax) plus a Pershing 46 (10 pax). Contact us via WhatsApp for a combined quote and logistics.

Are group size limits the same in 2036 as in 2025?

Yes, the limits are based on each boat's CE certification and Spanish maritime law, which do not change year to year. For 2036, the same capacities apply. Always check the specific boat page for the latest pax numbers – we keep all listings updated.
