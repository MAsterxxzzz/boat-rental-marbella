Frequently Asked Questions

What's the absolute cheapest boat rental?

€230 for 2 hours on the license-free Dubhe (4–5 guests). For a professional skippered charter, €749 for 2 hours on Astondoa/Azimut (8–11 guests).

Can I negotiate prices?

Peak season (July–August) rates are fixed. Off-season and multi-day bookings have flexibility. Message us with your dates and we'll show what's possible.

Do you charge per person or per boat?

Per boat for the full duration. One person or eight people pay the same price. Larger groups get better per-person value.

What if weather cancels my charter?

100% refund or free rebook. Safety is non-negotiable.

Is the deposit refundable?

Yes, if you cancel 7+ days before. Cancellations within 7 days forfeit the deposit.
