Choosing between a Marbella vs Fuengirola boat rental in 2029 comes down to style, budget, and what you want from your day on the water. Marbella's Puerto Banús is the undisputed luxury yacht hub of the Costa del Sol, while Fuengirola offers a more relaxed, family-friendly alternative. This guide compares the pros, cons, and real prices to help you decide which base suits your charter.

All our boats depart from Puerto Banús, Marbella – but we'll show you how Fuengirola stacks up if you're considering a different starting point. For a full overview of our fleet, visit our boat rental Marbella page.

Marbella Boat Rental: The Luxury Standard

Marbella, specifically Puerto Banús, is synonymous with high-end yachting. The marina itself is a destination: designer boutiques, Michelin-starred restaurants, and superyachts line the docks. Chartering from here gives you immediate access to the most exclusive stretch of the Costa del Sol coastline.

Pros: Unbeatable glamour, direct access to Cala del Faro and Cala Cortés, a wide choice of luxury yachts (up to 29m), and world-class beach clubs. Our Tier A boats – the Astondoa 40 and Azimut 39 – start at €749 for 2 hours (all-inclusive with skipper, fuel, drinks, snacks, and VAT). For a bigger experience, the Mangusta 80 (24m, 12 pax) costs €4,719 for a minimum 4 hours and includes a free jet ski.

Cons: Higher mooring fees, pricier dining, and more crowded marinas in peak season. Parking near Puerto Banús can be challenging – expect €30–€50 for a day in a secure lot.

Fuengirola Boat Rental: Value & Relaxation

Fuengirola, 20 minutes east of Marbella, offers a more laid-back boating experience. Its marina is smaller and less flashy, but it's well-equipped and family-oriented. The coastline here features sandy beaches, calm coves, and the popular Cabopino dunes.

Pros: Generally cheaper charter rates (especially for smaller boats), quieter anchorages, easier parking, and a more authentic Spanish feel. Fuengirola is ideal for half-day trips with kids or a relaxed sunset cruise without the Banús price tag.

Cons: Fewer luxury yacht options, no direct access to the swanky beach clubs of Marbella, and the marina lacks the high-end amenities of Puerto Banús. If you want a superyacht experience, Fuengirola can't compete.

Which Base Offers Better Anchorages?

From Marbella, you can reach Cala del Faro and Cala Cortés within 15–30 minutes – two of the most beautiful coves on the coast, with crystal-clear water and dramatic cliffs. Heading west towards Estepona opens up more secluded spots. From Fuengirola, the best anchorages are Cabopino (with its protected dunes) and the beaches near Mijas Costa. The water is generally calmer, but the scenery is less dramatic.

For snorkelling and cliff-jumping, Marbella wins. For a quiet family swim, Fuengirola is fine.

Nightlife & Dining After Your Charter

Puerto Banús is the epicentre of Costa del Sol nightlife. After a day on the water, you can dock and step straight into Pangea or Olivia Valère – or dine at a waterfront restaurant like Bibo by Dani García. Fuengirola offers a more casual scene: chiringuitos on the beach, tapas bars, and the lively Mijas Pueblo nearby. If you want to party until dawn, choose Marbella.

How to Get to Puerto Banús from Fuengirola

If you're staying in Fuengirola but want to charter from Marbella, it's a straightforward journey. By car, take the A-7 motorway (20–30 minutes). A taxi costs €30–€40. By boat, it's 8–10 nautical miles – a 25-minute ride on our Pershing 46 or Speedboat. We can arrange a transfer if you book a full-day charter.

Our Verdict: Which Should You Choose in 2029?

For a luxury experience, celebrity-spotting, and access to the best coves and beach clubs, book a boat rental in Marbella from Puerto Banús. Our fleet includes everything from the Azimut 39 (€749/2h) to the Ferretti 94 (29m, 12 pax – pricing on request). For budget-conscious families or a quiet day out, Fuengirola is a solid alternative – but you'll miss the glamour and variety that only Marbella offers.

All our charters include a licensed skipper, fuel, water, soft drinks, beer, white wine, cava, light snacks, insurance, safety equipment, and 21% Spanish VAT. No hidden fees. For 2029, we recommend booking at least 3–4 months ahead for summer dates.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, Marbella or Fuengirola boat rental?

Fuengirola is generally cheaper for bareboat or smaller skippered charters. However, our Marbella-based fleet offers competitive rates: Tier A boats (Astondoa 40, Azimut 39) from €749 for 2 hours, all-inclusive. For larger yachts (e.g., Mangusta 80 at €4,719 for 4 hours), pricing is similar regardless of base, but Marbella gives you more luxury options.

Can I charter a boat from Fuengirola with your company?

No – all our boats depart exclusively from Puerto Banús, Marbella. If you're staying in Fuengirola, you can drive 20–30 minutes to Puerto Banús or take a taxi (approx. €30–€40). Alternatively, consider a day charter that includes a stop at Fuengirola's coastline.

What are the best anchorages near Marbella vs Fuengirola?

From Marbella, top spots are Cala del Faro, Cala Cortés, and the coast towards Estepona. From Fuengirola, Cabopino and the coves near Mijas Costa are popular. Marbella offers more sheltered coves and a wider range of beach clubs.

Is Puerto Banús more expensive than Fuengirola marina?

Yes – mooring fees, fuel, and dining are notably higher in Puerto Banús. A coffee might cost €5 vs €2 in Fuengirola. However, the atmosphere, people-watching, and luxury yacht scene are unmatched. Fuengirola is more laid-back and budget-friendly.

Which base is better for a sunset cruise?

Marbella – especially from Puerto Banús. The sun sets over the Mediterranean with views of La Concha mountain. A 2-hour sunset charter (€749 on Tier A boats) is a classic experience. Fuengirola sunsets are lovely but lack the glamour of Banús.

How far is Fuengirola from Puerto Banús by boat?

Approximately 8–10 nautical miles, taking 25–40 minutes depending on your boat's speed. Our speedboats and Pershing 46 can cover it in under 20 minutes. It's an easy day trip if you want to visit both marinas.

Which has more nightlife after a boat charter?

Puerto Banús is the nightlife capital of the Costa del Sol, with clubs like Pangea and Olivia Valère. Fuengirola has a lively but more casual scene with beach bars and pubs. If you want high-end partying, choose Marbella.

What is the best boat for a family charter from Marbella?

The Azimut 58 Flybridge (17.6m, 12 pax) offers great space, a flybridge for sunbathing, and a comfortable saloon. For smaller groups, the Fairline Targa 12m (12m, 10 pax) is excellent. All include skipper, insurance, and refreshments.
