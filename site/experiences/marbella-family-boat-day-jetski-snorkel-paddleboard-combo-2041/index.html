Sample itinerary for your family boat day

Depart Puerto Banús at 10:00. Cruise east along the Marbella coastline, passing the Golden Mile and Marbella Marina. First stop: Cala del Faro – a sheltered cove perfect for snorkelling and paddleboarding. After an hour, head towards Cabopino for a jetski session (weather permitting). Lunch stop at a chiringuito (e.g., Elba Beach) or anchor for a swim. Return to Puerto Banús by 18:00. Your skipper can adjust the route to match your family’s preferences.

Best time of year for a family boat day in Marbella

The 2041 season runs from April to October. The warmest months are July and August, with sea temperatures averaging 24–26 °C and air temperatures around 30–35 °C. June and September offer slightly cooler conditions (air 25–30 °C, sea 22–24 °C) with fewer crowds. For the best snorkel visibility (10–15m), plan your trip between June and early September. Morning departures (10:00–12:00) give you calm seas and the best light for photos.

Safety and family-friendly features

All our boats carry life jackets for adults and children (sizes from 15 kg to 100 kg). The skipper holds a valid Spanish licence and will brief you on safety procedures before departure. The paddleboard and snorkel gear are suitable for ages 6 and up. Children under 12 must wear a life jacket at all times on deck. The jetski requires a valid boat licence (e.g., PNB or ICC) – if no one in your group has one, our skipper can drive while you ride as a passenger.

Frequently asked questions

What boats are available for the family boat day?

We offer the Astondoa 40 (12.5m, 9 guests) and the Azimut 39 (12.5m, 11 guests). Both depart from Puerto Banús and include a licensed skipper, fuel, drinks, snacks, insurance, and 21% VAT.

Is the jetski included in the price?

Yes, a jetski is included free of charge on the Mangusta 80 (Tier B, min 4h at €4,719). For the Astondoa 40 and Azimut 39 (Tier A), the jetski is available as an add-on at an extra cost – please enquire via WhatsApp for the exact rate.

Can we bring children on board?

Absolutely. Our boats are family-friendly, with life jackets for all ages. The Astondoa 40 and Azimut 39 have safe deck spaces and a bimini top for shade. Children under 12 must wear a life jacket at all times when on deck.

What snorkel spots do you recommend?

We typically head to Cala del Faro or Cala Cortés, both sheltered coves with calm, clear water. Snorkel gear (masks, fins, snorkels) is provided free of charge. The best conditions are in the morning before the sea breeze picks up.

Do we need a licence to drive the jetski?

Yes, Spanish law requires a valid boat licence (e.g., PNB or ICC) to operate a jetski. If no one in your group holds one, our skipper can drive the jetski while you ride as a passenger – or we can arrange a separate jetski rental with a guide.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 72+ hours before departure receive a full refund. Between 24 and 72 hours, a 50% refund applies. Less than 24 hours or no-show: no refund. Weather-related cancellations are fully refundable or reschedulable.

Can we customise the itinerary?

Yes, your skipper will plan the day with you. Typical stops include a snorkel spot, a paddleboard session near the coast, and a jetski run. You can also request a lunch stop at a chiringuito (e.g., Elba Beach or La Pesquera).

What should we bring on the day?

Bring swimwear, sunscreen, towels, sunglasses, and a hat. We provide all drinks, snacks, and safety equipment. If you want to listen to your own music, bring a Bluetooth speaker – our boats have AUX input too.
