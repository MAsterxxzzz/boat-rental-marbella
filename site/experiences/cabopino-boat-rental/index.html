Cabopino, Eastern Marbella boat rental · 12.5 m motor yacht · up to 9 guests

Cabopino is the gateway to the eastern Costa del Sol. It sits at the edge of Marbella's best dune beaches and snorkel anchorages. Perfect for guests staying in eastern Marbella, seeking quiet waters, or wanting license-free boat rentals. Morning seas here are calmer than afternoon (typical 14:00+ levante winds).

Why book from Cabopino, Eastern Marbella

  • 🏖️ Right by dune beaches and Cala Cortés snorkel anchorage
  • 🤐 Quietest departure point (fewer tourists)
  • 🆓 License-free boat rentals available
  • 🌊 Sheltered morning waters, calm mornings
  • 🚗 Easy 15-min drive from central Marbella

Boats available

Astondoa 40 and Azimut 39 — both 12.5 m motor yachts from €749/2h.

Typical itinerary

  1. Boarding at Cabopino Port (arrive 15 mins early)
  2. Eastbound cruise towards Estepona coastline
  3. First anchor at Cala Cortés snorkel site (30 mins in)
  4. Lunch on board or beach club stop
  5. Return to Cabopino by your chosen time

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to Cabopino Port (Puerto Deportivo Cabopino)?

Easy car access from central Marbella — approximately 12 km east of central Marbella. Parking available at the marina.

What's the best time to depart from Cabopino, Eastern Marbella?

Morning departures (09:00–11:00) offer the calmest sea conditions. Book your time slot when you contact us.

Can I extend my charter on the day?

Yes, if the boat isn't booked for a subsequent slot. Confirm with the skipper before your charter ends.
