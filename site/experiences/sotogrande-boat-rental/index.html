Sotogrande, Luxury Costa del Sol boat rental · 12.5 m motor yacht · up to 9 guests

Sotogrande is the most exclusive boat rental departure on the Costa del Sol. The marina itself is a luxury village with 5-star restaurants, boutique shopping, and a clientele of world-class yachts. Perfect for high-end groups, corporate charters, or guests staying in Sotogrande/Gibraltar area. The ultra-calm harbor waters and prestigious setting make this ideal for first-timers who want the full luxury experience.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get to Sotogrande Marina?

Easy car access from central Marbella — approximately 50 km west of central Marbella. Parking available at the marina.

What's the best time to depart from Sotogrande, Luxury Costa del Sol?

Morning departures (09:00–11:00) offer the calmest sea conditions. Book your time slot when you contact us.

Can I extend my charter on the day?

Yes, if the boat isn't booked for a subsequent slot. Confirm with the skipper before your charter ends.
