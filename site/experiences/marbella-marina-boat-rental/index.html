Central location boat rental · 12.5 m motor yacht · up to 9 guests · walking distance from Marbella old town

Marbella Marina (Puerto Deportivo Virgen del Carmen) is the most central boat rental pickup point on the Costa del Sol. Unlike Puerto Banús 7 km to the west, Marbella Marina sits directly on the old town promenade — a 5-minute walk from any hotel on the Golden Mile, and a flat stroll from the historic medina. Perfect for guests staying centrally who want to skip the car ride to Puerto Banús.

Marina location: Paseo Marítimo Rey de España, 29600 Marbella — right on the waterfront, between the old town and the port. Free public parking nearby (5 mins walk), or valet available. From €749 for 2 hours.

Why book from Marbella Marina

  • Central location — Old town hotels within walking distance (no taxi to Puerto Banús)
  • Easy arrival — Take the coastal promenade walk as part of your morning
  • Family-friendly — Shallow water, calm conditions in morning hours
  • No hassle parking — Ample free parking 5 mins away, or pay valet if you prefer
  • Flexibility — Morning departures avoid afternoon levante winds (typical 14:00 onwards)

Boats available from Marbella Marina

Our main 12.5 m motor yacht fleet works from Marbella Marina:

  • Astondoa 40 "Fufi" — Spanish-built, teak interior, up to 9 guests. Classic Mediterranean vibe.
  • Azimut 39 — Italian-built flybridge, up to 11 guests. Modern lines, upper sun-deck.

Both boats depart Marbella Marina daily at 09:00, 13:00, 16:00 and on-request times (subject to availability). Pricing starts at €749 for 2 hours, with fuel, skipper, drinks and VAT included.

Typical morning itinerary from Marbella Marina

  1. Boarding: Arrive 15 mins early at Marbella Marina pontoon. Welcome aboard, safety briefing, cold drinks.
  2. Westbound cruise: Head past the lighthouse, along the Golden Mile at 8-10 knots (calm morning sea).
  3. First anchor: Cala del Faro or Río Verde (30 mins in) — swim, snorkel, paddleboard.
  4. Optional lunch: Bring your own, or we can arrange a catered platter (€25–€60 pp).
  5. Return: Back to Marbella Marina by your chosen time (2h, 4h, 6h or 8h charter).

Parking and access

Free parking: Paseo Marítimo has ample free parking (blue zones, max 2h) 5 minutes' walk from the dock. Longer stays: paid car park 2 mins away (€1.50/hour or €12/day). Valet parking: Available on request through the marina office (€8).

Public transport: Marbella Marina is on the main bus route (Line 1 from the train station, 25 mins) and is 2 km from central Marbella (taxi €8–€10, 5 mins).

Marbella Marina vs Puerto Banús

Both marinas work perfectly — they serve different needs. Choose Marbella Marina if: You're staying in central Marbella, want to skip the car to Puerto Banús, prefer calm morning seas, or have mobility constraints (shorter walk). Choose Puerto Banús if: You want the largest fleet selection (30+ boats), prefer afternoon departures, or want to combine your charter with Banús dining and nightlife. Full comparison guide here.

Frequently asked questions

Can I modify the itinerary from Marbella Marina?

Yes — your skipper will adapt the route based on sea state, your group's preferences and the time of day. Morning charters from Marbella Marina typically head west (calmer water), while afternoon charters often go east towards Cabopino.

What if the weather is bad?

Skippers cancel at no cost when wind exceeds Force 4–5 (20+ knots). You're offered a refund or re-booking at no extra charge. Cancellations are usually called the evening before departure.

Is Marbella Marina good for first-timers?

Excellent for first-timers. The sheltered morning waters and shorter walk from central hotels make it ideal. Many guests prefer the calm morning sea to afternoon charter times.

Can I book a last-minute charter from Marbella Marina?

Often yes for same-day or next-morning departures, especially weekdays. Message us on WhatsApp — we confirm within 5 minutes if availability is there.

Other departure points

We operate from 5 marinas across the Costa del Sol: Puerto Banús (most boats), Marbella Marina, Cabopino (eastern beaches), Estepona and Sotogrande. Each has a different vibe and works best for different groups.
