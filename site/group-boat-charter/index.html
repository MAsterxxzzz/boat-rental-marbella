Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book a group charter?

3–4 weeks for most charters. Large corporate events (20+ people, multiple boats) need 6–8 weeks for event coordination and catering planning.

What if someone in our group can't swim or is nervous about water?

No problem. Life jackets available, crew will explain safety, and you can stay on the spacious deck. Our skipper is experienced with nervous guests and will make them comfortable.

Can we hire multiple boats for a very large group?

Yes! We can coordinate 2–3 boat charters for groups of 15–25+. Fleet formations, synchronized departures, and group activities arranged. Contact us for multi-boat events.

Do you offer team building packages or corporate discounts?

Yes! Corporate groups (8+ people) receive customized proposals with catering, team activities, and event coordination. Contact us to discuss corporate packages and group discounts.

Can we bring our own food/drinks on the charter?

Outside catering not permitted (safety & liability), but we offer comprehensive catering options at all price points. Ask about our catering partnerships for special diets or preferences.

What's the cancellation policy for group charters?

Full refund up to 7 days before charter. 50% refund 3–6 days before. No refund within 48 hours (weather cancellations always 100% refundable). Group deposit policies vary — discuss during booking.
