The Formula 1 Spanish Grand Prix returns in 2025, and Marbella is the natural base for fans who want race-day adrenaline mixed with Mediterranean luxury. With the circuit in Barcelona just a couple of hours away, the smart move is to book an F1 weekend boat rental in Marbella – watch the race live on board, then celebrate with a cruise along the Costa del Sol. Here's how to make it happen.

Why Marbella for F1 Weekend?

Marbella is the playground of the rich and famous, and F1 weekend amplifies that energy. Puerto Banús fills with supercars, celebrities, and party-goers, while the marina buzzes day and night. You can drive to the circuit for the race itself, but the real spectacle is back on the coast – and from a boat, you get the best seat in the house. Anchor off the Golden Mile, watch the race on a screen, then dive into the crystal-clear water.

How to Watch the Race from a Boat

You won't see the tarmac from the sea, but you can stream the race live on a tablet or TV while anchored in a calm bay. Bring your own device or ask us about onboard screens. The skipper will find a spot near Puerto Banús or Cala del Faro where the water is flat and the sun is warm. As the lights go out, raise a glass of cava – the sound of the engines is replaced by the gentle lapping of waves.

F1 Weekend Itinerary Ideas

Make the most of your charter with a structured day:

  • Morning: Depart Puerto Banús at 10:00, cruise to Cabopino for a swim stop.
  • Afternoon: Anchor for the race broadcast, enjoy snacks and drinks.
  • Late afternoon: Head to Estepona or Cala Cortés for a final dip.
  • Evening: Return to port, freshen up, and hit the Marbella nightlife.

An 8-hour charter covers all of this comfortably. For a shorter option, a 2-hour sunset cruise is perfect for post-race celebrations.

F1 weekend in Marbella is peak season. Boats sell out weeks in advance, especially the larger ones for groups. To secure your preferred vessel, contact us via WhatsApp or use the booking form on our main page. We'll confirm availability and lock in your slot.

Beyond the Race: Coastal Highlights

If you have extra days, extend your charter to explore the coast. Head to Sotogrande for upscale marinas, or the quieter Cabopino for a relaxed beach day. The views of La Concha mountain from the sea are unforgettable. Our skippers know the best anchorages – just ask.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is the F1 circuit from Marbella?

The Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya is about 90–120 km from Marbella, roughly a 1.5 to 2-hour drive depending on traffic. Many fans stay in Marbella for the glamour and nightlife, then drive up for the race. Alternatively, you can watch the race live on board your charter while enjoying the Mediterranean.

Can I watch the F1 race from a boat in Marbella?

You won't see the circuit from the sea – it's inland. But you can watch the race broadcast on a large screen or tablet while anchored off Puerto Banús, with the sound of waves and a cold drink in hand. After the race, cruise to a beach club or Cala del Faro for a swim.

Which boats are best for an F1 weekend party?

For groups up to 12, the Mangusta 80 (24m) is the ultimate party platform with a jet ski included. For smaller groups, the Astondoa 40 or Azimut 39 (up to 9–11 guests) are ideal at €749 for 2 hours. All include a skipper, drinks, and snacks.

What is included in the boat rental price?

Every charter includes a licensed skipper, fuel, water, soft drinks, beer, white wine, cava, light snacks, insurance, safety equipment, and Spanish IVA (21%). There are no hidden costs – just show up and enjoy.

How far in advance should I book for F1 weekend?

Book at least 4–6 weeks ahead. F1 weekend is one of the busiest of the year in Marbella, and our fleet of 17 boats fills quickly. Use WhatsApp for real-time availability and to secure your preferred boat and time slot.

Can we combine the F1 weekend with a day trip to Cabopino or Estepona?

Absolutely. A 4-hour charter from Puerto Banús can take you to Cabopino or along the coast to Estepona. For a longer trip, an 8-hour charter lets you explore the Golden Mile, stop for lunch at a chiringuito, and return for the evening's parties.

Are there any age restrictions for the jet ski on the Mangusta 80?

The jet ski on the Mangusta 80 is available for guests aged 16 and over with a valid ID. A safety briefing is given, and life jackets are provided. Use is at the skipper's discretion based on sea conditions.
