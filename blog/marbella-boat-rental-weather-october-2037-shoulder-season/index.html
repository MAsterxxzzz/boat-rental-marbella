Read on for weather data, pricing, boat recommendations, and practical tips to make the most of your Marbella boat rental this October.

Why October Shoulder Season Works

Summer in Marbella runs June through August – peak heat, peak prices, peak crowds. September holds the warmth but still busy. October flips the script: daytime temperatures hover 24–26°C, the sea sits at 22°C, and the marina at Puerto Banús is noticeably quieter. You'll find better availability on boats and often more flexible scheduling. For a Marbella boat rental October 2037 shoulder season experience, this is the sweet spot.

October Weather & Sea Conditions

Expect an average of 9 hours of sunshine per day. Rain is minimal – typically 3–4 days for the whole month. Winds are light, usually 5–10 knots, making for calm cruising along the Costa del Sol. Water temperature of 22°C means swimming is comfortable without a wetsuit. Evening temperatures drop to around 18°C, so bring a light jacket for the return trip.

Frequently asked questions

Is October a good month for a boat rental in Marbella?

Yes. October is shoulder season: sea temperatures average 22°C, air temperatures 20–26°C, and you get about 9 hours of sunshine. Crowds are thin, marinas are quiet, and prices are lower than July–August. It's ideal for a relaxed day on the water.

What is the weather like in Marbella in October 2037?

Expect daytime highs of 24–26°C, cooling to 18–20°C in the evening. The sea is still swimmable at 22°C. Rain is rare – typically only 3–4 days in the month. Winds are light, making for calm cruising conditions.

Which boats are available for October charters?

Our entire fleet is available: from the Astondoa 40 (€749/2h) to the Mangusta 80 (€4,719/4h). All depart from Puerto Banús. Popular choices for smaller groups include the Azimut 39 and the Fairline Targa 12m. For larger parties, the Ferretti 94 or Maiora 26m are excellent.

What is included in the charter price?

Every booking includes a licensed skipper, fuel, water, soft drinks, beer, white wine, cava, light snacks, insurance, and safety equipment. Spanish IVA (21%) is already included. No extra charges at the marina.

Can we anchor and swim in October?

Absolutely. The sea is warm enough for swimming until late October. Popular spots include Cala del Faro, Cala Cortés, and the coves near Cabopino. Your skipper will choose the best anchorage based on wind and swell.

How do I book a boat for October 2037?
